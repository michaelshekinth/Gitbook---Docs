# SOLar Mission

SOLar Mission is a web-based blockchain crash game built for the Kingdom Coin (KC) ecosystem on the Solana blockchain. It’s inspired by games like _Space XY_ and designed for fast, competitive, and provably fair gameplay, with direct integration into KC’s staking and deflationary mechanics.

Players connect their Solana wallet, pay a small KC entry fee, and bet on how far a rocket will travel before it explodes. Cashing out before the crash secures profits, while waiting too long means losing the stake for that round.

#### **Where to Find the Game**

* Website: SOLkingdom.com _(launch domain for all KC ecosystem games)_
* Available inside the Games section of the SOLkingdom portal alongside Degen Trader.
* Playable on desktop and mobile browsers — no app download required.
* Requires a Solana wallet (e.g., Phantom, Solflare, Backpack) connected to the website to participate in real mode.

### **How to Play**

1. **Connect Wallet**
   * Log in to **SOLkingdom.com** and connect your Solana wallet.
   * Ensure you have enough KC to enter (12.5 KC per session).
2. **Join a Round**
   * Pay the entry fee in KC.
   * 10% is burned immediately; 90% goes to the prize pool and ecosystem treasury.
3. **Rocket Launch**
   * The rocket takes off and a multiplier counter starts (e.g., 1.00x, 1.25x, 3.50x...).
   * The multiplier increases until the rocket “crashes” at a random point determined by on-chain verifiable RNG.
4. **Cash Out or Risk It**
   * You can cash out anytime before the crash to secure your winnings (stake × multiplier).
   * If the rocket crashes before you cash out, you lose that round’s stake.
5. **Points & Rewards**
   * Final winnings in KC are converted to a score, which becomes points (1:1).
   * Points are added to your monthly total to boost staking APR (8% base → up to 13%).

#### **Game Modes**

**Demo Mode (Free Play)**

* Purpose: Let new players experience SOLar Mission without financial risk.
* No wallet connection required.
* Starts with virtual KC balance for practice.
* No rewards or burns — purely for learning gameplay.
* Available at all times, ideal for onboarding and tutorials.

**Real Mode (KC-Powered)**

* Entry Fee: 12.5 KC per round (\~$0.50 at launch price).
* Burn: 10% of entry fee removed from circulation.
* Rewards: Winnings added to score → converted to staking points.
* Competition: Contributes to monthly leaderboards (global, country-specific).
* Tokenomics Impact: Generates burns, prize pool funding, and KC demand.
