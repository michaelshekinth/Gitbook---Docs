# Math Calculation with Tokenomics

#### **Annual Burn Rate:**

Every session burns 1.25 KC. With an estimated **209,000 plays per day** (380k users \* 0.55 plays/day), the yearly burn from gameplay is about **95 million KC** (209k \* 365 \* 1.25 KC). Additionally, the ecosystem plans for cosmetic enhancements (e.g. skins costing 476 KC each with 20% burned) which add roughly **39 million KC** burned per year. Together this yields about **134 million KC burned annually** from play and enhancements, supporting a net deflationary pressure on the 1 billion KC supply (≈13.4% of the total supply burned per year if those play rates hold). This aligns with the documentation’s projection of “\~100M KC/year” net burn (the slight difference accounting for new issuance via rewards).

#### **Staking Interest Pool Sustainability**

Kingdom Coin allocates 25% of its total supply (250,000,000 KC) to an interest-bearing rewards pool for staking. This pool is used to pay the base 8% APR and any booster APR earned through game points. Under the tier system, not all players will reach the maximum 13% APR; an average APR of \~10% is a reasonable assumption (midway in the 8–13% range, given many will be at base and some at higher tiers). The documentation’s math model (using an initial staked amount of \~200M KC growing 4% annually and \~10% average APR payout) shows that the **interest pool would last roughly 7–9 years**. In a sample projection, paying out \~20 million KC in the first year (10% of 200M) and increasing payouts as more tokens get staked, the 175M–250M pool would be nearly depleted by year 8 on the current trajectory. This matches the white paper’s conclusion that the staking rewards pool can sustain about **8+ years of interest** before running dry (with the exact duration depending on user growth and average APR). The tiered APR system is capped at 13% max boost, ensuring the payout rate cannot grow unbounded beyond that. In practice, the combination of the token burn (reducing circulating supply) and the fixed reward pool means the system is designed so that by the time the interest pool is exhausted (year \~8), a significant portion of KC will have been burned or locked up, ideally increasing the token’s value and allowing governance to decide on further measures.
