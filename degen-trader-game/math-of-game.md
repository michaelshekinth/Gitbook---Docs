# Math of Game

### **Preparing for Battle**

Before stepping into either arena, you swap a bit of SOL for KC tokens.\
If you’ve got 500 KC or more, congratulations — you’re already auto-staked at 8% APR. But the real magic? Your APR can climb to 13% if you perform well in the games.

### **Scene 2: Entering SOLar Mission (Crash Game)**

You decide to start with the space mission.

* Entry fee: 12.5 KC.
* The system instantly burns 10% (1.25 KC) — gone forever, making KC rarer.
* The remaining 11.25 KC? That’s split between prize pools, the treasury, and development.

You begin with 2,000 in-game points for the session. Each quick round in the crash game tests your reflexes — cash-out too early and you miss the multiplier, too late and your ship explodes.

When the session ends, whatever points you have left are converted 1:1 into Kingdom Points. These count toward your APR boost.

### **Scene 3: APR Tier Ladder**

Every month, you can earn up to 50,000,000 points per game (SOLar Mission & Degen Trader). The higher your total points, the bigger your boost:

| Tier | Monthly Points | APR Boost       |
| ---- | -------------- | --------------- |
| 1    | 0–1M           | +0% (8% total)  |
| 2    | 1M–5M          | +1% (9% total)  |
| 3    | 5M–10M         | +2% (10% total) |
| 4    | 10M–25M        | +3% (11% total) |
| 5    | 25M–100M       | +4% (12% total) |
| 6    | 100M+          | +5% (13% total) |

Important rule: For Tiers 4–6, at least 20% of your points must come from each game. No one-game domination allowed.

### **Scene 4: Optional Power-Ups**

Want your ship to stand out? Buy a skin for 476 KC.

* 20% burned (95.2 KC).
* 80% (380.8 KC) goes to the treasury and utility.\
  If \~410,000 skins sell in a year, that’s 39M KC burned from cosmetics alone.

### **Scene 5: Entering Degen Trader**

Now you head to the market floor — Degen Trader.

Same entry fee: 12.5 KC → burn 1.25 KC → 11.25 KC to pools.

You start with $2,000 virtual capital and 30 turns (days) ( Customizable ) to survive the chaos: Elon tweets pump coins, rug pulls wipe out projects, IRS letters hit your desk — every decision counts.

At the end of 30 days ( Just for an example ) , your portfolio value in USD is converted 1:1 into Kingdom Points. Same 50M point cap per month per game, same tier ladder, same 20% multi-game rule.

### **Scene 6: The Kingdom’s Economy in Motion**

Now here’s the magic — **KC is deflationary**.

From gameplay alone:

* 380,000 players × 0.55 sessions/day = \~209,000 plays/day.
* Burn per play = 1.25 KC → 261,250 KC burned/day.
* That’s 95,356,250 KC burned/year just from entry fees.

**From enhancements:**

* Skins burn \~39M KC/year.

**Total annual burn:** \~**134.36M KC/year** — all while prize pools and staking keep rewarding the best players.

### **Scene 7: How Points Boost Your Earnings**

Let’s say you staked **10,000 KC**:

* **Tier 3 example:** You earn 6M points in Degen Trader and 1.3M in SOLar Mission → 7.3M total → Tier 3 (+2% boost) → APR = 10% → \~83 KC earned monthly (compounding).
* **Tier 5 example:** 12M from SOLar Mission, 18M from Degen Trader → 30M total → Tier 5 (+4% boost) → APR = 12% → \~500 KC/month on a 50,000 KC stake.

### **Scene 8: Your Role in the Kingdom**

Every session you play, every skin you buy, every risky cash-out choice you make — they all move the KC economy.\
You’re not just playing for fun — you’re strengthening a deflationary token, growing your stake, and climbing the ranks.

The SOLkingdom isn’t just a game.\
&#xNAN;_**It’s an economy, an adventure, and a competition — and every KC counts.**_
