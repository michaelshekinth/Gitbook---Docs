### **Degen Trader – Game Overview**

Degen Trader is a web-based blockchain trading simulation game that reimagines the classic _Drug Wars / Dope Wars_ formula in a crypto context.\
Instead of dealing drugs, players trade virtual cryptocurrencies (BTC, ETH, SOL, DOGE, PEPE, etc.), navigating volatile markets and random events to grow their portfolio from $2,000 starting capital to potentially tens of millions in in-game value.

It’s built for the Kingdom Coin (KC) ecosystem on Solana, meaning:

* Game sessions are paid for in KC.
* Points earned directly boost staking APR.
* Entry fees and purchases feed into the burn + prize pool system.

### **Core Gameplay Loop**

1. **Connect Wallet**
   * Player connects a Solana wallet (Phantom, Solflare, etc.) to the game site.
   * Swaps SOL → KC if needed.
2. **Pay Entry Fee**
   * 12.5 KC ($0.50 at launch) for one session.
   * Session simulates 30 in-game days.
   * 10% burn (1.25 KC) + 90% to prize pool/development.
3. **Trading Phase**
   * Players can buy and sell from a list of meme coins and major cryptos.
   * Prices change every in-game day, randomly within **1x–10x** range (rare up to 100x).
   * Strategic buying/selling is key — players can hold, dump, or diversify.
4. **Random Events**
   * Inspired by crypto culture & real-world drama:
     * **Elon Tweet** → DOGE/BONK pumps.
     * **Rug Pull** → coin price goes to 0.
     * **IRS Raid** → forced tax payment.
     * **Stablecoin Depeg**, **Meme Coin Hype**, etc.
   * Some events require player decisions (e.g., avoid scam or risk big loss).
5. **Progression**
   * Start in “Bedroom” market with basic coins.
   * Upgrade to “Yacht” or “Dubai Penthouse” with higher net worth → unlock better trading options & lower fees.
6.  **Session End**

    *   After 30 in-game days:

        * Final portfolio value = score.
        * Score → 1:1 Points toward staking APR boost.
        * Max 50M points per game per month.

### **Economy & Integration with KC**

* **Entry Fee Burn:** Every play removes KC from supply.
* **Points System:** Drives staking APR boosts (8% → 13% cap).
* **Enhancements:** Skins/avatars purchasable in KC (20% burn).
* **Leaderboard Rewards:** KC prizes from 75M KC pool.
* **Multi-Game Requirement:** For high APR tiers, must earn points from Degen Trader **and** SOLar Mission.
