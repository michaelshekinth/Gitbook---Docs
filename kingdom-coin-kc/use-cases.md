# Use Cases

### **Primary Use Cases**

* **Game Entry & Participation**
  * Entry fee for games like **Degen Trader** and **SOLar Mission** is **12.5 KC (\~$0.50)**.
  * 10% of entry fees are burned (reducing supply), while 90% goes to prize pools and development.
  * Enhancements (e.g., skins, cosmetic upgrades) are purchased in KC, with 20% burned.
* **Staking & Passive Income**
  * KC is automatically staked upon acquisition (min. 500 KC).
  * Base APR:  **8%**, boostable up to **13%** via a **Points System** linked to game performance.
  * Points are earned at a 1:1 ratio from final game scores and are capped at 50M per game/month to encourage multi-game play.
* **Points System & Tier Rewards**
  * Higher scores = higher tiers = higher APR.
  * Example: Tier 6 requires **100M+ points/month** for a +5% boost (max APR 13%).
  * At least 20% of points for high tiers must come from each available game.
* **Competitions & Leaderboards**
  * 75,000,000 KC prize pool for monthly/quarterly competitions.
  * Rewards for top global players, top country players, and “All-Time Heroes.”
  * Tied to games and referral performance.
* **Referral Rewards (Virtual BTC Program)**
  * Send meme-themed virtual NFTs with referral links to friends.
  * Successful referrals reward **100 KC to the new user** and **50 KC to the sender**.
  * Referrals also earn points toward staking APR boosts.
* **Token Burns & Deflation**
  * Estimated \~134M KC burned annually from game entries and enhancements.
  * This creates a **net deflationary supply** despite staking rewards, supporting long-term value.
* **Governance (Future DAO)**
  * Planned governance features for stakers (min. 500 KC) to vote on ecosystem decisions, game features, and reward structures.
