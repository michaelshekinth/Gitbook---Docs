# The Distribution

**Total Supply and Distribution:** Kingdom Coin has a fixed supply of **1,000,000,000 KC**. The allocation is designed to support the game-centric ecosystem:

* **Game Rewards & Utility:** 25% (250,000,000 KC) set aside for staking interest payouts and game prize pools. This is the source for the 8%-13% APR rewards and competition prizes.
* **Airdrop (Community Incentives):** 25% (250,000,000 KC) for airdrops to early users, beta testers, and possibly referral rewards.
* **Marketing:** 10% (100,000,000 KC) for marketing efforts.
* **Liquidity:** 10% (100,000,000 KC) for initial DEX/CEX liquidity provisioning, to ensure smooth trading of KC.
* **Presale:** 14% (140,000,000 KC) allocated to a presale event to raise initial funds and distribute tokens to investors.
* **Founders:** 8% (80,000,000 KC) for the founding team (vested over 2 years).
* **Advisors & Partners:** 8% (80,000,000 KC) for advisors and ecosystem partners (vested as well).

These sums indeed add up to 100% (250M + 250M + 100M + 100M + 140M + 80M + 80M = 1,000M). The smart contract design respects these allocations by not minting new tokens arbitrarily; all interest and prizes come from the allotted pools, and burns permanently reduce supply.
