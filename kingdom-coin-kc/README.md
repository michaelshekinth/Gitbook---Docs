# Kingdom Coin - KC

### **About Kingdom Coin (KC)**

Kingdom Coin (KC) is the native DeFi utility token powering the SOLkingdom ecosystem — a collection of web-based blockchain games, staking systems, and referral programs — all built on the Solana blockchain for speed, low fees, and scalability.

KC has a fixed total supply of 1,000,000,000 tokens and integrates deeply into every aspect of the ecosystem:

* Gaming Currency – KC is the entry fee for flagship games like Degen Trader and SOLar Mission, and future titles released every \~2 months.
* DeFi Staking Asset – KC holders can stake their tokens for a base APR of 8%, with boosts up to 13% APR based on monthly in-game performance (points system).
* Community Reward Token – KC fuels competitions, referral rewards, airdrops, and limited-time events.
* Deflationary Asset – Through automatic burns (e.g., 10% of all game entry fees, 20% of enhancement purchases), KC’s circulating supply decreases over time.

KC acts as the economic backbone of the ecosystem, ensuring that gameplay, staking, and community engagement are directly tied to token demand and scarcity.
