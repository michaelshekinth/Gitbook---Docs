# How it works

## **Step-by-Step**

#### 1. **Get Your Referral Link**

* Any user with a connected wallet can generate a unique referral link via the **Referrals Dashboard** on SOLkingdom.com.
* This link embeds your wallet ID for tracking.

#### 2. **Share the Link**

* Share it on social media, chats, forums — anywhere.
* The platform encourages meme-based sharing: users can generate memes or NFT-style referral banners directly from the site using their referral code embedded.

#### 3. **Track Signups**

* When someone clicks your link and connects their wallet, they become your referral.
* The system attributes any KC they buy/earn in the future (during the referral period) to your Virtual BTC earnings.

#### 4. **Earn Virtual BTC**

* The doc gives an example structure:
  * **Direct referral**: You get X Virtual BTC for each KC they spend or stake.
  * **Tiered referrals**: Some campaigns may allow 2nd-level earnings (friends of friends).
* Virtual BTC accrues in your dashboard in real time — shown as a growing BTC balance.

#### 5. **Convert to KC**

* At predefined claim windows (weekly/monthly), you can convert your Virtual BTC to KC.
* **Conversion rate** is fixed for the campaign (e.g., 0.0001 Virtual BTC = 1 KC — example only, exact rate is set in campaign rules).
* When you claim:
  * A percentage of the KC is immediately **burned** (to stay in line with tokenomics deflation goals).
  * The rest is credited to your KC wallet.
* This burn mechanism prevents the referral program from being inflationary.
